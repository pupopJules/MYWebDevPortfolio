About

I'm a full-stack developer who works comfortably across the whole request path — from schema design and API architecture through to the interface someone actually clicks on. I like projects that force trade-offs: performance vs. simplicity, speed vs. correctness.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of clean backend systems and interfaces that don't get in the way. I write JavaScript and TypeScript daily, I'm comfortable owning infrastructure, and I care more about a product working reliably than about it looking clever on a resume.
